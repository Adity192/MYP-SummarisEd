What is MYP SummarisEd?

MYP SummarisEd is a free compiled study hub designed specifically for students in the IB Middle Years Programme. The platform organises Mathematics and Sciences resources by subject and unit, giving students instant access to videos, notes, past papers and revision materials that are fully aligned with the official MYP syllabus. No more wasting time searching the internet for relevant resources — everything you need is in one place.
